Who it is for

Clinics and dental practicesSalons, gyms and yoga studiosRestaurants, cafés and bakeriesRetail shops, jewellers and garages

What is included

Claim and verify the profile

We claim your listing or create it, finish Google's verification and fix the basics: name, category, phone, timings and address.

Services, products and photos

We add your real services, price lists where they help, and 10 or more photos taken at your place, not stock pictures.

Reviews, the honest way

A QR card for your counter and a short WhatsApp message to ask happy customers for a Google review. We help you reply to every review. We never write fake ones.

Posts and offers

Regular Google posts for offers, festivals and new arrivals, so the profile never looks abandoned.

Call, directions and WhatsApp

Call, directions and WhatsApp buttons set up and tested, so one tap becomes one enquiry.

Local keywords

The words customers really use, in English, Marathi and Hindi, placed in your profile and website where they belong.

A monthly report

Views, calls, direction requests and what we will change next month, in plain language.

What to expect

Most profile improvements start to show in 3 to 6 weeks. Where you appear on the map depends on the competition around you, your reviews and how complete your profile is, so we never promise a position.

What we need from you

  • Your business name, logo and phone number
  • A few photos, if you have them
  • Access to your Google Business Profile, or the Gmail it was created with

What we will never do

  • Fake reviews or paid reviews
  • Fake addresses or virtual offices
  • A promised ranking or "page one" guarantee

Which plan includes it

Included from the Aarambh plan, ₹5,999 a month. Ad spend, where it applies, is separate and paid by you directly to Google and Meta. We add no commission.

Questions about Google Maps & Local SEO

Do I need to spend on ads for Google Maps?

No. Google Maps and local search work is free of ad spend. Your plan fee covers our time to set it up and look after it.

What if someone else already claimed my listing?

We help you request ownership through Google's own process. How long that takes is up to Google, and we keep you updated.

How many reviews do I need?

There is no magic number. In our free audit we treat a 4.2 star rating with 25 or more reviews as a healthy local benchmark, and we show you how to get there with real customers.
