Meta Lead Campaigns

Who it is for

Clinics and diagnosticsGyms, coaching classes and schoolsBuilders and interior designersJewellers, boutiques and wedding services

What is included

An ad account in your name

You own the account and the data. We work inside it, and you can see every rupee spent.

Local targeting

Areas, radius and audiences chosen around where your customers actually live and travel.

Creative in your customers' language

Ad copy and visuals in Marathi, Hindi or English, built around a clear local offer.

Lead forms or Click-to-WhatsApp

Short forms that ask only what you need, or ads that open a WhatsApp chat with a ready message.

Conversions API and Pixel tracking

Tracking that tells Meta which clicks became real enquiries, so the ads learn and you can see the true cost of each enquiry.

Weekly improvements

We check the numbers every week, pause what is not working and put money behind what is.

A monthly report

Leads, cost per lead, calls and what changes next, shared on WhatsApp and as a PDF.

What to expect

First enquiries often arrive within 7 to 14 days. Results become steadier by month 2 or 3, after the first round of optimisation. For one shop or clinic we suggest starting with ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 a month in ad spend. Below ₹5,000 the ads rarely get enough data to learn.

What we need from you

  • Your offer, price or package you want to promote
  • Access to your Facebook page and ad account, or help creating them
  • Someone who can reply to enquiries quickly, ideally within 15 minutes

What we will never do

  • Take a commission or markup on your ad spend
  • Promise a fixed number of leads
  • Buy followers, likes or fake engagement

Which plan includes it

One managed campaign from the Shuru plan, ₹9,999 a month. Meta and Google together from the Vikas plan, ₹19,999 a month. Ad spend is separate. Ad spend, where it applies, is separate and paid by you directly to Google and Meta. We add no commission.

See all plans and prices

No GST is added to our fees. We are not registered for GST at present, and GST is charged only once our business is registered.

Questions about Meta Lead Campaigns

Is the ad budget included in the plan fee?

No. Your ad budget is separate and paid by you straight to Meta, from your own account. We take no commission and add no markup.

Is ad spend charged per post or per reel?

No. It is one total monthly budget for all your ads together. The plan fee covers the work of making and managing them.

What is Conversions API?

It is a way of sending real enquiry data back to Meta, so the ads learn which clicks turn into customers. Without it, the ads are guessing.
