In short
  • Month-to-month, no lock-in, cancel any time.
  • Your ad accounts, pages, content and customer data stay yours.
  • No guaranteed results. Clear tracking and monthly reports instead.
  • We keep your business and customer information confidential and secure.

1. Parties and this agreement

This agreement is between you (the "Client", named on the signed order form) and the three founders of the mediagraphy.in (KDMC Growth Lab), Ayush Sahu, Prakhar Dubey and Nikhil Kushwaha, of Kalyan East, Maharashtra, together "we". We are not registered as a company, LLP or firm at present, so we sign as individuals trading under this name.

This agreement, the order form for your plan, our Refund and Cancellation Policy and our Privacy Policy together make up the whole deal. If they conflict, the order form comes first, then this agreement.

2. Services

We provide the services listed for your plan on the pricing page and the order form. Anything outside the plan needs your written approval and an agreed fee before we start. We use reasonable skill and care and may use trusted tools and freelancers, but we remain responsible for the work.

3. Start, term and renewal

The agreement starts on the start date in the order form, after you accept it and pay the first invoice. It runs month to month until either of us ends it under the Ending the agreement clause. We send a fresh invoice for each period. Nothing renews by auto-debit.

4. Fees and payment

Fees are in Indian rupees and payable in advance for each month, quarter or year by UPI or bank transfer, within 3 days of the invoice. If an invoice is more than 7 days overdue, we may pause work after a written reminder until it is paid. We do not charge penalty interest.

We are not registered for GST at present, so no GST is charged. If we become liable to register, we will tell you in advance and GST will appear as a separate line on invoices from that date.GST is added as a separate line on every invoice. Fees are quoted before any tax you must deduct at source (TDS). If you deduct TDS, pay it to the government and send us the certificate promptly.

Third-party costs (for example a domain, hosting or stock licences) are charged only with your prior approval and shown on the invoice.

5. Ad spend and ad accounts

Ad accounts, pages, pixels and payment methods are in your name. You pay Google, Meta and other platforms directly. We take no commission and add no markup on ad spend. We will not spend beyond the monthly budget you approve in writing, and we will tell you before any change. Platform charges and taxes billed by the platform are yours.

We work through role-based access you grant us, such as Meta Business partner access or a Google Ads manager link. Never share passwords or OTPs. You can remove our access at any time.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate information, access and approvals in time, ideally within 2 working days. Delays move our timelines.
  • Own or have the right to use everything you give us: logos, photos, music, testimonials, prices and customer details.
  • Make sure your offers, claims and ads are true and lawful, and that you hold every licence or registration your business needs. This includes industry rules such as medical and clinic advertising rules, MahaRERA registration numbers for real estate, FSSAI for food, and education and coaching advertising rules.
  • Give your own privacy notice and obtain the consents you need when you collect customers' data through ads, forms, WhatsApp or your website.

7. Content, approvals and our right to refuse

We share creative work for your approval before it goes live, with the number of revisions stated in your plan. Extra revisions are by agreement. We will not make fake reviews, false claims, misleading before-and-after results or content that breaks the law or platform rules. We may refuse or pause such work, and we will explain why.

8. Ownership and use of work

On full payment of the fees for the work, we assign to you the copyright in the content we create for you under this agreement (posts, reels, videos, photos, graphics, landing pages and ad creatives). The assignment is worldwide and for the full period of copyright. The parties agree that section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 does not cause the assignment to lapse. Moral rights stay with their creators as the law provides.

Third-party material (stock images, fonts, music, templates) stays under its own licence. We tell you where a licence limits use. Our own tools, templates, methods and know-how remain ours, and you get the right to use their output.

Unless you ask us in writing not to, we may show finished work in our portfolio. We never show your confidential numbers or customer details without your consent.

9. Reviews and endorsements

We help you collect genuine customer reviews and never write or buy fake ones. Where an ad or post carries a testimonial or influencer endorsement, it must be genuine, and any material connection must be disclosed as the Indian advertising and consumer-protection rules require.

10. No guaranteed results

We do not guarantee any number of enquiries, calls, visits, sales or rankings. Estimates are guides. Outcomes depend on your offer, price, location, competition, season, reply speed and platform decisions. We promise honest work, tracking in accounts you own, a plain monthly report and a weekly call as your plan states.

11. Confidentiality

Each of us keeps the other's non-public business information confidential, uses it only for this agreement, and shares it only with people who need it and are bound to keep it secret. This does not cover information that is public, already known to the receiver, independently developed, or that the law requires us to disclose. The duty continues for 2 years after the agreement ends, and for as long as a trade secret stays secret.

12. Customer data and security

You are the Data Fiduciary for your customers' personal data collected through ads, lead forms, WhatsApp and your website. We act as your Data Processor, only on your written instructions and only to deliver the services. We will:

  • use that data for no other purpose and never sell it;
  • keep it secure, with access limited to people who need it, two-step verification and no shared passwords;
  • use only sub-processors needed for the service (for example Google, Meta and WhatsApp) and remain responsible for them;
  • tell you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware, of a personal data breach affecting your data, so you can meet your own duties;
  • help you respond to your customers' rights requests;
  • return or delete the data within 30 days after the agreement ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Google, Meta and other platforms are independent controllers of the data they collect, under their own terms.

13. Warranties and limit of liability

We will perform the services with reasonable skill and care. Beyond that, to the extent the law allows, we give no other warranty, including that any platform will approve your ads or keep your accounts active.

To the extent the law allows, neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit, and our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim arose. These limits do not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

14. Indemnity

You will cover us against claims and reasonable costs arising from material, claims or instructions you gave us that were unlawful or infringed someone's rights. We will cover you against claims and reasonable costs arising from our own infringement of someone's rights in work we created, other than from material you supplied. The party seeking cover must tell the other promptly and let it help defend the claim.

15. Ending the agreement

You may end it any time by written notice (see the Refund and Cancellation Policy). We may end it by 7 days' written notice, with a refund for any period we have not delivered.

Either of us may end it immediately by written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 7 days of notice, or if continuing would be unlawful. We may end it immediately if you ask us to do something unlawful or misleading, or if there is abuse or harassment of our team.

When it ends: unpaid fees for work done remain payable, we hand over access and files within 7 days, we send a final report, and we remove our own access. The clauses that by their nature continue (ownership, confidentiality, data, liability and disputes) continue.

16. Things outside our control

Neither of us is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond reasonable control, such as natural disaster, power or internet failure, government action, or a platform's outage, policy change or account suspension. We will tell you quickly and do what we reasonably can to carry on.

17. Other work and exclusivity

This agreement is not exclusive. We may work for other clients, including businesses like yours, but we will not use your confidential information for them.

18. Changes and transfer

Changes must be in writing and agreed by both sides. A confirmation by email or from the WhatsApp number on the order form counts as writing. Neither of us may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent. If we later register as a company, LLP or firm, we will ask you in writing to agree to move the agreement to that entity on the same terms.

19. Notices

Notices may be sent by email to the address on the order form and to growth@themediagraphy.in, or by WhatsApp to the numbers on the order form, and are treated as received on the day sent, if sent on a working day, otherwise the next working day.

20. Governing law and disputes

Indian law applies. We will first try to settle any dispute by talking, for up to 30 days after written notice of it. If that fails, either of us may start mediation by a recognised mediator, or go to court. The courts at Kalyan, Maharashtra have jurisdiction, subject to any right you have under consumer law to approach a Consumer Commission where you live or work.

21. Electronic acceptance and stamp duty

The agreement is valid when accepted electronically under the Information Technology Act, 2000, for example by signing and returning the PDF, by an e-sign, or by written acceptance from the email address or WhatsApp number on the order form. Electronic records of the acceptance may be used as evidence.

If stamp duty is payable under the Maharashtra Stamp Act, 1958, we will share it equally and arrange it before the agreement is relied on in court. An instrument that is not duly stamped may not be admitted as evidence until the duty and any penalty are paid.

22. Whole agreement and severability

This is the whole agreement about its subject and replaces earlier talks and promises about it. If a clause is found invalid or unenforceable, the rest continues. If one of us does not enforce a right straight away, they do not lose it.
