1. Who we are

the mediagraphy.in (KDMC Growth Lab, "we", "us") is a small independent digital marketing and content studio run by its three founders, Ayush Sahu, Prakhar Dubey and Nikhil Kushwaha, in Kalyan East, Maharashtra, India. We are not registered as a company, LLP or firm at present.

For personal data collected through this website we decide why and how it is used, so we are the "Data Fiduciary" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). You are the "Data Principal".

Contact: growth@themediagraphy.in · +91 86377 44905 · Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM IST.

2. Laws we follow

We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 with the Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 with the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

The 2025 Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and most duties for businesses start from 13 May 2027. We follow them now instead of waiting.

3. What we collect

Only what you choose to give us:

  • Your name, mobile number and business name, type and area.
  • What you type in the message box and your meeting choices (day, time, mode of meeting, topics, and your quiet hours if you share them).
  • Answers you enter in the free audit and lead estimator, such as business type, area, enquiries a month and what you already have in place.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us: passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar or PAN numbers, card or bank details, UPI PINs, health records, biometric data or anyone's children's data.

If you become a client and pay by UPI or bank transfer, we see the payer name, UPI or account reference and amount that your bank shows us. We never hold your card details.

4. What we do not collect

This website has no analytics, advertising pixels, session recorders, social-media plug-ins or third-party fonts and scripts. Fonts and images load from our own site. We do not build a profile of you and we do not track you across other websites.

5. How the free tools work

The audit, estimator, plan finder and creative checker do their sums inside your own browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to send them.

When you tap a button such as "Send audit on WhatsApp", "Confirm on WhatsApp", "Call me back" or "Send", WhatsApp opens with a message already written. Nothing reaches us until you press Send in WhatsApp. At that moment, the details you typed in that form may also be saved in a private enquiry log (a Google Sheet that only the founders can open) so we can follow up.

6. Why we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and call you back.
  • To book, confirm or change a meeting. A time you pick on our website is only a request until we confirm it with you on WhatsApp.
  • To prepare and send the report or estimate you asked for.
  • If you become a client, to deliver the service, send invoices, keep accounts and meet our legal duties.
  • To keep this website secure and working.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by tapping the button after seeing this notice, and the purposes the law allows without consent, such as a legal duty or the exact purpose for which you voluntarily gave us the data. We use it for nothing else.

We send promotional messages only if you ask for them. You can say STOP at any time and we will stop.

7. Who sees it and who we share it with

Only the three founders see your enquiry details. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

We share data only with the services we use to do our work, and only as much as needed:

  • WhatsApp (Meta), the app you use to message us.
  • Google, for the private enquiry log (Sheets and Apps Script), email, and Google Calendar if you tap "Add to Google Calendar".
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and may log technical details such as IP address and browser type to run and secure the service.
  • Our accountant or other professional adviser, if needed for tax or legal compliance, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Google and Meta advertising tools, only for clients and only inside the client's own ad accounts.

We may also disclose data when a court, police authority or other body with legal power lawfully requires it.

8. Data stored outside India

Google, Meta and GitHub run servers in several countries, so some data may be processed outside India. The DPDP Act allows this unless the Government of India restricts a country. We choose well-known providers and share the minimum.

9. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: deleted within 12 months after our last contact, or earlier if you ask.
  • Clients: for as long as we serve you, and after that for the time tax, accounting and contract law require us to keep invoices and records.
  • WhatsApp chats stay on the founders' phones. We delete a chat's personal details when you ask and the law does not require us to keep them.

When a retention period ends, we delete or anonymise the data.

10. How we protect it

  • This website is served only over HTTPS and uses a strict Content Security Policy, so it cannot load code from anywhere else.
  • No third-party scripts or trackers run on any page.
  • Data you type is cleaned and length-limited before it is stored, and the enquiry log is private.
  • Only the three founders have access, with two-step verification on our Google, GitHub and email accounts.
  • We collect the minimum, and we delete data when it is no longer needed.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act and Rules require, and report cyber incidents to CERT-In as its directions require.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it.
  • Ask us to erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. It is as easy to withdraw as to give. If you withdraw, we may no longer be able to provide a service that needs the data.
  • Use our grievance process below.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act for yourself.

Write to growth@themediagraphy.in or message us on WhatsApp from the number you gave us. We may ask you to confirm your identity first so nobody else can get your data. We acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 30 days, and in every case within the time the law allows.

You also have duties: give us accurate details, do not pretend to be someone else, and do not give us another person's data without their permission.

12. Grievances and complaints

For any concern about your personal data, write to the founders' grievance desk at growth@themediagraphy.in, or call +91 86377 44905 (Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM IST). If we do not resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is functioning, after using our process first, as the DPDP Act provides.

13. Children

Our website and services are for business owners aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us data, tell us and we will delete it.

14. If you are a customer of one of our clients

When we run ads or lead forms for a client, the client decides why and how their customers' data is used, and we act only on the client's written instructions as their processor. Please contact that business first about your data. We will help them respond.

15. Cookies and storage

We set no cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps one small setting, your light or dark theme choice, on your own device only. Clear it any time from your browser settings. Sites we link to, such as WhatsApp or Google, have their own policies.

16. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when something changes. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we will tell you before it takes effect, by WhatsApp or email if we hold your details.

Questions? growth@themediagraphy.in or WhatsApp us. This page is general information for our clients and visitors, not legal advice.
