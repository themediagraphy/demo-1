Two towns, two catchments

A customer in Badlapur seldom shops in Ambernath. We plan one profile, one ad radius and one set of landmarks for each town, rather than treating the stretch as a single market.

A practical way to begin

Our estimates treat this belt as a lower-cost ad market. Many shops here can start with the Aarambh or Shuru plan and add ads when enquiries are ready to be handled, which keeps early spend small.

Commuter households

Many residents travel to Kalyan, Thane or Mumbai for work, which means they search and message in the evening and on weekends. Posting, offers and replies timed for those hours reach them when they are free to act.

Questions about Ambernath & Badlapur

Do you cover Ambernath and Badlapur as one market?

No. They are two towns, so we set a separate Google profile, ad area and local wording for each. If you have a branch in both, each branch gets its own profile.

Is it worth running ads in a smaller town?

Often yes, and at a lower cost per enquiry, but start with a complete Google profile and a quick WhatsApp reply. Ads only help once you can answer enquiries well.

How far should the ad radius reach?

We set it around where your customers actually live and travel. In smaller towns that can be wider than in a dense city, and we narrow it if the numbers show people are not coming from far.
