Questions we hear from local business owners

Straight answers about plans, pricing, GST, tracking and how we work. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Working with us

Do you work with small shops?

Yes. Most of our clients are local owners. We'll tell you honestly if a plan is too big for you.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your Google and Meta accounts, so you can see every rupee.

How fast can we start?

Most clients go live within 10 days of the first meeting.

Can we meet in person?

Of course. We come to your shop anywhere in Kalyan, Dombivli, Ulhasnagar, Titwala or nearby, and chai is on us. You can also choose a phone or video call.

Who will I talk to?

A real campaign manager from our team, on WhatsApp or call, Monday to Saturday. No bots and no call centre.

What do I need to give you to start?

Your business name, logo, phone number, a few photos if you have them and access to your Google and Meta pages. We guide you through each step and it takes about an hour.

Will you create content in Marathi and Hindi?

Yes. Posts, reels, ad copy and WhatsApp messages can be in Marathi, Hindi or English, whichever your customers use.

Do you handle Google reviews and my Maps profile?

Yes. We set up and verify your Google Business Profile, add photos and offers, and help you collect more genuine reviews with a QR card. We never write fake reviews.

Do you work with businesses outside Kalyan and Dombivli?

Yes. We mainly serve Kalyan, Dombivli, Ulhasnagar, Titwala, Ambernath, Badlapur and Thane. We can also run Google and Meta ads for nearby areas.

What if my competitors are also advertising?

That is normal in KDMC. We focus on a clear local offer, good photos, fast replies and the right area targeting, so you do not need to outspend them.

Pricing and billing

What does it cost to get started?

The audit and the first meeting are free. Plans start at ₹5,999 a month. Your Meta and Google ad spend is separate and paid by you directly to the platforms.

Do you give invoices? Is GST charged?

Yes, every payment gets a clear invoice or receipt and a monthly report. We are not registered for GST at present, so the prices shown are what you pay. GST is charged only once our business is registered for it, and when that happens it will be shown as a separate line on your invoice and told to you in advance.GST is added to our fees as a separate line on every invoice, so you always see exactly what you pay.

Do you take a commission on ad spend?

No. Your ad budget goes straight from your own account to Google and Meta. We never take a cut or add a markup. You pay our plan fee and nothing else.

How much should I spend on ads as a small shop?

Start with ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 a month for one shop or clinic. Below ₹5,000 the ads do not get enough data to learn. We will tell you honestly if a smaller budget is better spent on Google Maps and WhatsApp first.

Is the suggested ad spend per post or per reel?

No. It is one total monthly budget for all your ads together, paid directly by you to Meta and Google. It is not charged per post or per reel. Your plan fee covers the work of making and managing everything.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month. Quarterly and yearly billing only give you a discount. You can stop at any time with notice, and your ad accounts always stay yours.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can move up when you are ready to grow or step down in a slow season. The change applies from your next billing month.

Results and tracking

How will I know it is working?

You get a plain-language report every month and a weekly call. Enquiries are tracked in accounts you own, so you can check every rupee.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can promise exact numbers. We promise clear tracking, fast fixes and straight talk about what is and is not working.

How long before I see enquiries?

Google Maps improvements usually show in 3 to 6 weeks. Meta lead campaigns can bring the first enquiries within 7 to 14 days, and become steadier by month 2 or 3 after the first optimisation.

What is the difference between a reel and an on-site shoot?

A reel is one short vertical video that we edit with captions and local-language text. An on-site shoot is when our team visits your shop, clinic or site and records photos and many clips in one visit, which we then turn into reels, posts and ad creatives.

What is a CRM and do I need one?

A CRM is a simple list or app where every enquiry is saved with its status, so no customer is forgotten. Small shops can start with a sheet. Bigger teams get a proper CRM set up in the Shikhar plan.
