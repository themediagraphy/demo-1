Working with us

Do you work with small shops?

Yes. Most of our clients are local owners. We'll tell you honestly if a plan is too big for you.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your Google and Meta accounts, so you can see every rupee.

How fast can we start?

Our target is to go live about 10 working days after the first meeting, once we have your Google and Meta access and your approvals. We confirm the real date with you in writing at the first meeting, because it depends on how quickly access and photos reach us.

Can we meet in person?

Of course. We come to your shop anywhere in Kalyan, Dombivli, Ulhasnagar, Titwala or nearby, and chai is on us. You can also choose a phone or video call.

Who will I talk to?

A real campaign manager from our team, on WhatsApp or call, Monday to Saturday. No bots and no call centre.

What do I need to give you to start?

Your business name, logo, phone number, a few photos if you have them and access to your Google and Meta pages. We guide you through each step and it takes about an hour.

Will you create content in Marathi and Hindi?

Yes. Posts, reels, ad copy and WhatsApp messages can be in Marathi, Hindi or English, whichever your customers use.

Do you handle Google reviews and my Maps profile?

Yes. We set up and verify your Google Business Profile, add photos and offers, and help you collect more genuine reviews with a QR card. We never write fake reviews.

Do you work with businesses outside Kalyan and Dombivli?

Yes. We mainly serve Kalyan, Dombivli, Ulhasnagar, Titwala, Ambernath, Badlapur and Thane. We can also run Google and Meta ads for nearby areas.

What if my competitors are also advertising?

That is normal in KDMC. We focus on a clear local offer, good photos, fast replies and the right area targeting, so you do not need to outspend them.

What is included in the free audit?

You answer about 20 quick questions and tick what you already have in place. You get a score, a category-by-category breakdown, a 30-day plan and the monthly upside you may be missing. It takes about two minutes, needs no sign-up and costs nothing.

What is the free chai and strategy meet?

A 30-minute conversation about your numbers, with no sales pitch. We come to your shop for chai, or we can talk on a call or video. The time you pick on our website is a request. We confirm or change it with you on WhatsApp.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM IST. Message us on WhatsApp any time and we reply first thing in the next working window.

Pricing and billing

What does it cost to get started?

The audit and the first meeting are free. Plans start at ₹5,999 a month. Your Meta and Google ad spend is separate and paid by you directly to the platforms.

Do you give invoices? Is GST charged?

Yes, every payment gets a clear invoice or receipt and a monthly report. We are not registered for GST at present, so the prices shown are what you pay. GST is charged only once our business is registered for it, and when that happens it will be shown as a separate line on your invoice and told to you in advance.GST is added to our fees as a separate line on every invoice, so you always see exactly what you pay.

Do you take a commission on ad spend?

No. Your ad budget goes straight from your own account to Google and Meta. We never take a cut or add a markup. You pay our plan fee and nothing else.

How much should I spend on ads as a small shop?

Start with ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 a month for one shop or clinic. Below ₹5,000 the ads do not get enough data to learn. We will tell you honestly if a smaller budget is better spent on Google Maps and WhatsApp first.

Is the suggested ad spend per post or per reel?

No. It is one total monthly budget for all your ads together, paid directly by you to Meta and Google. It is not charged per post or per reel. Your plan fee covers the work of making and managing everything.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month. Quarterly and yearly billing only give you a discount. You can stop at any time with notice, and your ad accounts always stay yours.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can move up when you are ready to grow or step down in a slow season. The change applies from your next billing month.

Which plan should I choose?

Use our free audit and plan finder. As a rule, Aarambh suits a single shop that wants to be found on Google Maps, Shuru adds content and one Meta campaign, Vikas adds Google and Meta ads management with tracking, and Pragati and Shikhar add landing pages, on-site shoots and automation. We will tell you honestly if a smaller plan fits you better.

How do I pay?

By UPI or bank transfer against an invoice, in advance for the month, quarter or year you choose. We do not set up auto-debit or store your card details, and every payment is one you make yourself.

Results and tracking

How will I know it is working?

You get a plain-language report every month and a weekly call. Enquiries are tracked in accounts you own, so you can check every rupee.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can promise exact numbers. We promise clear tracking, fast fixes and straight talk about what is and is not working.

How long before I see enquiries?

Google Maps improvements usually show in 3 to 6 weeks. Meta lead campaigns can bring the first enquiries within 7 to 14 days, and become steadier by month 2 or 3 after the first optimisation.

What is the difference between a reel and an on-site shoot?

A reel is one short vertical video that we edit with captions and local-language text. An on-site shoot is when our team visits your shop, clinic or site and records photos and many clips in one visit, which we then turn into reels, posts and ad creatives.

What is a CRM and do I need one?

A CRM is a simple list or app where every enquiry is saved with its status, so no customer is forgotten. Small shops can start with a sheet. Bigger teams get a proper CRM set up in the Shikhar plan.

What is in the monthly report?

Leads, cost per lead, calls, what worked, what we changed and what we will do next month, in plain language. You get it on WhatsApp and as a PDF, and we go through it with you on the weekly or fortnightly call your plan includes.

Google Maps and local SEO

What is a Google Business Profile and why does my shop need one?

It is your free listing on Google Search and Maps, with your address, timings, photos, reviews and call and direction buttons. When someone searches "near me", it is often the first thing they see. A complete, active profile is the base of local marketing for any shop, clinic or class.

How do you get a shop into the Google Maps 3-pack?

Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. We set the right categories and services, add real photos, keep your name, address and phone consistent, post regularly, build genuine reviews and add local pages. Nobody can pay for a place or promise a position, and distance is outside anyone's control.

How long does Google Business Profile verification take?

Google decides the method, such as a video, phone or email check, and the time it needs. Often it takes a few days, and sometimes longer if Google asks for more proof. We guide you through every step and keep you updated.

Can you remove bad Google reviews?

We can flag reviews that break Google's policies, such as spam, fake or off-topic ones, and Google makes the final call. We cannot remove honest negative reviews, and we never write or buy fake reviews. The best answer to a bad review is a polite reply and more genuine happy customers.

How do I get more genuine Google reviews?

Ask at the right moment, right after good service. A QR card at the counter and a WhatsApp link make it easy, and replying to every review shows you care. Do not offer gifts or discounts in exchange for 5-star reviews, because that breaks Google's rules and can get reviews removed.

What is local SEO and how is it different from ads?

Local SEO earns free, organic visibility in Google Search and Maps for searches near the customer. It takes weeks to build and keeps working. Ads buy visibility straight away for as long as you pay. Most local businesses do best with both, starting with the Google profile because it needs no ad spend.

Do I need a website if I already have a Google Business Profile?

You can start without one. A fast mobile page with a WhatsApp button still turns more visitors into enquiries, gives your ads somewhere to send people and lets you show offers and services properly. Many shops begin with the profile and add a page later.

Instagram, Facebook and Google ads

Should a small local business run Instagram ads or Google ads?

It depends on how your customers decide. People who need something now, like a dentist or an AC repair, search on Google. People who buy on impulse or by look, like a salon, restaurant, boutique or gym, respond to Instagram and Facebook. Our audit points you to the right mix, and small budgets often start with one.

What is a Meta lead campaign?

It is an ad on Instagram and Facebook that lets people send an enquiry through a short lead form or open a WhatsApp chat with you. The leads arrive in your own account, and we track them from the ad to the enquiry so you can see what each lead costs.

What is click-to-WhatsApp advertising?

An ad with a button that opens a WhatsApp chat with your business. It suits India well because many customers prefer to chat. We set up the greeting message and quick replies so every chat starts with the right questions.

What is the Conversions API and why does it matter?

It lets your website, form or CRM send enquiry events back to Meta, so tracking stays more reliable when browsers or apps block the usual pixel. Better data helps Meta find similar people. We set up Pixel and Conversions API tracking from the Vikas plan.

Do I control my ad budget?

Yes. You set the monthly budget in your own ad account. We recommend how to use it and only spend within the amount you approve in writing. You can see every rupee in your own Ads Manager.

Why are my ads not getting leads?

The usual reasons are an unclear offer, the wrong area or radius, weak photos, a slow reply, tracking that is not set up, or an ad that has not finished learning. Our audit checks these one by one, and we fix them in order of impact.

How fast should I reply to an enquiry?

Enquiries go cold quickly, so we recommend replying within 15 minutes during working hours. Auto-replies and saved answers on WhatsApp Business help you acknowledge at once, and then you follow up personally.

Can you advertise a clinic, coaching class or real estate project?

Yes, within the rules. Medical practice, real estate and education advertising have strict rules, for example on claims and on showing a MahaRERA registration number. We keep ads factual and tell you if a claim looks risky. You remain responsible for your licences and for proving your claims.

Websites, landing pages and WhatsApp

What is a landing page and when do I need one?

A landing page is one focused page for a single offer, such as a free trial class or a check-up, with a clear WhatsApp button or form. When ads send people there instead of a busy home page, more of them take action. It is included from the Pragati plan.

Why does my website need to be fast on mobile?

Most local customers browse on a phone, often on 4G and in a hurry. A slow page loses them before they see your offer. We build light, mobile-first pages with a WhatsApp button on every page.

Who owns my website, domain and content?

You do. The domain and ad accounts are in your name, the content we create is assigned to you once fees are paid, and we hand over access if you leave. Our own templates and tools stay ours.

What is WhatsApp lead routing?

It sends each enquiry from your ads, website and Google profile to the right person on your team on WhatsApp, with auto-replies, labels, catalogue and away messages. It means no enquiry sits unanswered in one person's phone.

Do you use the WhatsApp Business app or the API?

Most small shops start with the free WhatsApp Business app, with its catalogue, quick replies and labels. Bigger teams may move to the WhatsApp Business API with a CRM, which we set up in the Shikhar plan. Meta charges for some API messages, and you pay that to Meta directly.

Can you send WhatsApp broadcasts to my customers?

Only to people who have agreed to hear from you, and following WhatsApp's own rules. We do not buy or use bought contact lists, and every broadcast must let people opt out.

Content, reels and shoots

What does an on-site shoot include?

Our team visits your shop, clinic or site, records photos and many clips in one visit, and then turns them into reels, posts and ad creatives. The Pragati plan includes one shoot a month and the Shikhar plan includes two.

Do you shoot outside Kalyan?

We come to Kalyan, Dombivli, Ulhasnagar, Titwala and nearby areas as part of our normal visits. For places further away, we agree the plan and any travel cost with you first, and show it on the invoice.

Can I use my own photos and videos?

Yes. Send them on WhatsApp or by a shared folder and we edit them into posts and reels. Please make sure you own the rights to them and to any person or music in them.

Do you use stock or AI-made images for my business?

We prefer real pictures of your real shop, because local customers can tell the difference. If we ever use a stock or AI-made image for a background or a festive creative, we tell you, and we never pass it off as your real premises.

How often should I post on Instagram?

Steady beats frequent. A realistic rhythm you can keep up works better than bursts. Our plans start at 8 posts a month and go up to 12 posts plus 4 reels a month, and we plan them around your offers and local festivals.

Privacy, security and your contract

Is your website safe to use? Do you track me?

The site loads only over HTTPS, runs no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and uses a strict security policy so it cannot load code from elsewhere. The tools calculate inside your browser. Data leaves your device only when you tap a send or book button.

How do you protect my business and customer data?

We collect the minimum, limit access to the three founders with two-step verification, keep the enquiry log private, use role-based access to your ad accounts and never ask for passwords or OTPs. If something goes wrong we tell you quickly and report as the law requires. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Do you follow India's data protection law?

Yes. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules were notified on 13 November 2025, and most duties for businesses start on 13 May 2027. We already follow them: a clear notice, consent, minimum data, deletion on request, a grievance contact and breach reporting.

Is there a written contract?

Yes. Before any paid plan begins, we share a Service Agreement and an order form with your plan, fees and start date. It is month to month with no lock-in, and nothing is charged until you have read and accepted it. Read the standard terms on our Service Agreement page.

Who owns my ad accounts, content and customer data?

You do. Ad accounts, pages and your customers' data stay in your name, the content we make for you is assigned to you once fees are paid, and we only use customer data to deliver your service.

What happens to my data if I stop working with you?

We hand over access and files within 7 days of your request, send a final report and remove our own access. We return or delete your customers' data within 30 days after the agreement ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

What is your refund and cancellation policy?

You can cancel any time, with no lock-in or penalty. A month that has started is not refunded, a month we failed to deliver is credited or refunded, and early exit from a quarterly or yearly plan is settled at the monthly rate. Refunds go back within 7 working days. See the full Refund and Cancellation Policy.

Are you a registered company?

We are a small independent studio run by three founders, and we are not registered as a company, LLP or firm at present. Contracts are made with the founders together, and we will tell you in writing if that changes. We are also not registered for GST at present.We are registered for GST and show it on every invoice.

How do I report a security problem or make a complaint?

Write to growth@themediagraphy.in or message us on WhatsApp. For a security issue, share it privately, access only what you need to show it, and give us time to fix it. For a data or service complaint, we acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 30 days.
